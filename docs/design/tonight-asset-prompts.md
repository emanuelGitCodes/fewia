# Tonight prototype food assets

The built-in Image Generation tool created these illustrative food photos.
The originals were converted to WebP at 1200 pixels wide, quality 86.
The final files have adjacent JSON files that contain their exact prompts.
They do not represent verified cooking results or serving quantities.

## bean-tacos

File: `frontend/public/prototype/bean-tacos.webp`

Generate a photorealistic editorial food photograph for a clean modern meal-planning app. A close three-quarter overhead composition of three simple corn tortillas filled with black beans, small diced white onion and red salsa on a warm ivory speckled ceramic plate. These are the only visible food ingredients; no lime, cilantro, cheese, meat or garnish. Natural soft window light, warm but accurate colors, light stone tabletop, inviting freshly cooked food with real texture. Frame the plate closely with ample food detail, a quiet uncluttered background, landscape 4:3 composition that also crops beautifully to a portrait center. No hands, cutlery, text, logos, labels or interface. This is illustrative recipe photography, not an advertisement.
